# Product vision: Your Quality-Gated AI Career Agent

> Mirrored from the Obsidian vault (*AI-ENGINEER OS* → `Automations/job-applyier/AI career agent.md`), which is the source of truth. Update the note first, then sync here.

An autonomous career agent for ambitious professionals who want a serious job search without turning it into a second full-time job.

For a one-year-experience AI engineer, it continuously finds relevant roles, decides whether each is genuinely worth pursuing, creates a truthful role-specific application, and submits it automatically. It then manages the surrounding work: tracking outcomes, organizing interviews, following up, and helping you improve over time.

The goal is not "apply everywhere." It is: never miss a strong opportunity, never send a generic application, and never lose track of what happens next.

## Core value proposition

> "Set your career direction once; your AI Career Agent works every day to move you toward it."

It gives you:

- Continuous opportunity discovery across selected job sources.
- Selective, high-confidence auto-application within your rules.
- Resume and application tailoring that remains accurate to your real experience.
- A single record of every opportunity, application, response, and interview.
- A calmer, more consistent job search with minimal daily effort.

## Ideal first user

The launch persona is an early-career AI engineer with roughly 1–3 years of experience who:

- Is actively seeking AI engineer, machine learning engineer, applied AI, LLM, data/AI, or adjacent software roles.
- Has a credible but still developing portfolio and needs each application to communicate it well.
- Wants strong opportunities, not indiscriminate volume.
- Is busy building skills, working, or interviewing and cannot monitor job boards constantly.
- Is comfortable delegating applications, provided the agent respects clear guardrails.

This is intentionally a focused initial audience. Their roles require nuanced matching between skills, projects, tools, domain exposure, location, and seniority — exactly where generic auto-apply products feel weak.

## The Quality Gate

Before submitting, the agent checks five dimensions:

1. **Eligibility** — location, work authorization, remote/hybrid preference, compensation floor, experience range, and job type.
2. **Role fit** — match to your target titles, AI/ML stack, domain interests, and career direction.
3. **Credibility** — whether your real resume, projects, and achievements can support a compelling application without exaggeration.
4. **Opportunity quality** — company reputation, role clarity, growth potential, application freshness, and warning signs.
5. **Application quality** — tailored resume, relevant project emphasis, required questions completed, and no conflicting or incomplete information.

Only roles that pass the agreed threshold are submitted. Borderline roles are held for review; poor-fit roles are quietly skipped with a reason.

## End-to-end user journey

### 1. Define your career target

You tell the agent what "good" means:

- Target roles: AI Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, ML Engineer.
- Preferred industries, company types, and locations.
- Remote or hybrid preferences.
- Salary expectations and non-negotiables.
- Skills and projects to emphasize.
- Roles or companies to avoid.
- Daily or weekly application volume limit.

The agent turns this into a living "career brief," not a static search filter.

### 2. Build your professional narrative

Instead of treating your resume as a document, the agent understands it as a set of credible career stories:

- Technical strengths and tools.
- Projects and measurable outcomes.
- Domains you care about.
- Achievements you can confidently discuss in an interview.
- Growth areas to position honestly.

This prevents it from tailoring applications in ways you cannot defend later.

### 3. Search continuously and decide selectively

Each day, the agent scans for new roles, removes duplicates and stale listings, scores fit, and prioritizes jobs where you are both qualified and motivated.

You receive a concise daily update: applications submitted, best opportunities found, roles held for review, and reasons for skipped roles.

### 4. Tailor and apply autonomously

For approved-fit jobs, the agent creates a role-specific version of your resume and application narrative. It highlights the projects, AI tools, and outcomes most relevant to that job — without inventing experience.

Applications are submitted automatically, and every action is recorded in your timeline.

### 5. Manage the response loop

When a recruiter responds, the agent classifies the message and takes the next appropriate action:

- Flag an interview request immediately.
- Create a calendar event and reminders.
- Surface required preparation materials.
- Draft a response when one is needed.
- Remind you about follow-ups or deadlines.
- Update the application status automatically.

### 6. Learn from outcomes

Over time, the agent identifies patterns:

- Which titles, companies, and skills generate responses.
- Which resume narratives perform best.
- Where your profile is being filtered out.
- Which gaps are worth addressing through a project, portfolio update, or skill-building plan.

The job search becomes smarter, not merely busier.

## Core capabilities

- Personalized opportunity discovery and matching.
- Autonomous, quality-gated application submission.
- Truthful per-role resume and application tailoring.
- A live application pipeline with status, notes, and history.
- Recruiter-message detection and follow-up management.
- Interview scheduling, calendar events, reminders, and preparation prompts.
- Daily digest and weekly job-search performance review.
- Adjustable autonomy: job criteria, quality threshold, application limits, exclusions, and pause controls.
- Career insight layer that turns outcomes into practical next actions.

## Differentiators

| Pain point | Differentiated experience |
| --- | --- |
| Generic auto-apply damages credibility | A credibility guardrail only uses experience you can genuinely stand behind. |
| Job boards create noise | A quality gate optimizes for worthwhile opportunities, not raw application count. |
| Users fear losing control | Every submission has an explainable decision record: why it qualified, what was tailored, and what was sent. |
| Applications disappear into a void | The agent tracks the full lifecycle, including recruiter messages, interviews, and follow-ups. |
| Early-career candidates struggle to tell a coherent story | A career-narrative layer consistently connects projects, skills, and outcomes to each role. |
| Job hunting is emotionally exhausting | The agent provides calm progress visibility: "Here is what moved forward while you lived your life." |

## Moments that delight

- **Morning momentum:** "While you were offline, I found 18 roles, skipped 14, held 2 for your review, and submitted 2 high-fit applications."
- **Explainable confidence:** Every application shows a simple fit rationale, such as: "Strong match: LLM application development, Python, RAG project relevance, and acceptable experience range."
- **Interview readiness at the right moment:** When an interview is booked, the agent presents the job summary, likely discussion areas, your strongest relevant examples, and a preparation checklist.
- **No-ghosting follow-up:** If an application has been silent for an appropriate period, it prompts or prepares a thoughtful follow-up.
- **Career momentum view:** A weekly summary shows applications, responses, interviews, highest-performing narratives, and one concrete improvement opportunity.

## Positioning

Not another job board. Not a resume generator. Not a spammy auto-apply tool.

This is a personal AI Career Agent: a selective, always-on job-search partner that represents the user thoughtfully and moves their career forward autonomously.

Your Quality-Gated AI Career Agent finds the right jobs, tailors truthful applications, and submits them automatically — then manages every response, interview, and follow-up. Built first for early-career AI engineers, it replaces exhausting job-board routines with a transparent, high-quality career autopilot.
